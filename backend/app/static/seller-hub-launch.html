Point your camera at a card and let assisted recognition do the first pass. Check the exact printing, language and grade before it goes into your collection. Less manual entry; more time for the hobby.

Know the market

Understand what a card may be worth using available, clearly labelled market references. Exact edition, language and grade matter; missing values stay unverified.

04 /PLANNED

Sell with Drop Rate

Prepare a listing once, then work towards selling eligible items through the Drop Rate store, eBay and Whatnot where integrations permit. You approve the details; we aim to make the admin feel effortless.

05 /IN DEVELOPMENT

Sales without the spreadsheets

Follow orders, seller fees and payout progress in one place as the full selling workflow rolls out, with every physical item and owner accounted for.

06 / NEXT UPPLANNED CONCEPT

Live Auction Lens

Watching a card auction? We're exploring an optional pop-up that uses screen recording, with your permission, to recognise the card in view and show a relevant market-value reference. So you can judge whether the live bid looks under, around or over market value before you bid.
