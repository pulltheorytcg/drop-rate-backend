LORCAST
Factual English printing metadata is obtained through the documented Lorcast
API: https://lorcast.com/docs/api

collector numbers, language, rarity and release dates. No images, rules,

CARD COLLECTOR CAPITAL — CC BY 4.0
Creator and source: Card Collector Capital, https://cardcollectorcapital.com/data
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Dataset: https://cardcollectorcapital.com/data/bowman-checklist-lookup-2026-10-27.json
Reviewed version: 27 October 2026.
This is a selected 1,722-row compilation from three Bowman checklists, not
complete coverage of sports cards, all Bowman products or all parallels.
Drop Rate renames and filters fields into its metadata schema. Original
record IDs, manufacturer evidence URLs, source pages and review dates remain
with each record. No prices or artwork are imported. A checklist entry does
not verify a physical card's exact parallel, language, condition or
authenticity. CC BY 4.0 covers the compilation and original annotations, not
the linked third-party documents, trademarks or artwork.

CARD COLLECTOR CAPITAL — 2026 TOPPS HERITAGE — CC BY 4.0
Creator and permission: Card Collector Capital, https://cardcollectorcapital.com/data
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Dataset: https://cardcollectorcapital.com/data/2026-topps-heritage-short-prints.json
Reviewed dataset version: 30 September 2026; access review: 4 November 2026.
This separate source contains 100 numbered starred-base checklist references,
not all 400 base cards, every Topps product, every parallel or a Panini database.
Drop Rate filters and renames fields, joins subjects on multi-player cards
without creating extra cards, and preserves checklist categories, source pages,
manufacturer evidence URLs and the source's #360 Back Variation warning.
The compilation and original annotations are CC BY 4.0; linked third-party
documents, trademarks and artwork are not relicensed. Only the reviewed JSON
is fetched; no manufacturer PDF, images, prices or print-run estimates are
downloaded or inferred. A source checklist does not confirm physical language,
parallel, grade, authenticity or ownership. Changes to this pinned export need
another source review. The existing Bowman source remains separate and intact.

INDIVIDUALLY REVIEWED FIGURE STARTER
Four Funko product references contain individually checked factual product
names, item numbers, box numbers and franchise/product-line identifiers.
Evidence URLs and review dates accompany the records. They are a small
manually curated starter, not a complete Funko database, an approved Funko API,
or permission for automated extraction. No Funko website is fetched by this
importer. No images, descriptive marketing text, prices, release dates or
unconfirmed chase/exclusive editions are copied or inferred. Existing Good
Smile references remain available separately; this import does not duplicate
or delete them. Confirm the edition/variant against the physical item.
