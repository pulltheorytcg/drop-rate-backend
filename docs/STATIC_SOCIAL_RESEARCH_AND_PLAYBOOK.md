# Drop Rate: static social research and automation playbook

Research cut: 4 October 2026. Founder coverage updated at 01:27 BST; multi-daily goal reaffirmed at 01:42 BST; five platforms confirmed at 01:44 BST.
Working branch: `docs/daily-tcg-editorial-20261004`; draft PR #512.

## Decision and scope

**The deliverable is a running organic marketing n8n machine, not a content plan.**
It must research, create original branded content, manage publication across the
channels, post multiple times daily, measure audience/store results and adapt
future choices. This report and the preparation component are implementation
inputs, not completion of that goal.

Confirmed starting target: THREE distinct core posts per day across Instagram,
Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and X. Proposed test times are 10:00, 14:00 and 19:00
Europe/London. This means FIFTEEN platform publication slots per day. Twelve are
across four static-capable candidate platforms; the remaining YouTube slots need
the static publishing gap resolved. Counts are targets, not verified deliveries.
One story syndicated across channels is still one story, not four experiments.

The daily loop should research at 06:00, 09:00, 13:00 and 18:00; select before each
slot; render and preflight; publish; reconcile actual visibility; then measure at
24 hours and seven days. A weekly bounded learning job adjusts topic, hook,
format and timing weights while preserving franchise coverage. These are proposed
inactive schedules, not running jobs. Ordinary validated posts should not require
founder approval. Automatically skip unqualified stories or use still-valid
fallbacks; escalate only unresolved access problems or consequential exceptions.

Channel management covers queues, platform-specific captions and links, token
health, media checks, failed/partial delivery, corrections and analytics. Replies,
DMs, giveaways and moderation need separate explicit rules; they are not secretly
included in posting permission. Keep a rolling one-day publishing queue initially
so a late source correction can stop unreleased variants.

Build a recognisable collecting and entertainment publication that brings relevant
audiences to Drop Rate. Artist stories are one recurring format, not the whole
account. The founder explicitly wants One Piece, Pokemon, Dragon Ball, Naruto,
Comics, Manga, Riftbound, Lorcana, anime, live-action releases and comic-book films.
Keep the immediate production scope static images and multi-slide carousels,
with no music. Video is a later, separate decision.

Model franchise, medium and format independently. One Piece can support cards,
manga, anime or live-action stories; Marvel/DC can support comics and films;
Disney can support Lorcana and screen releases. A film announcement is not card
market evidence. Card grading logic must never silently apply to comics.

The commercial hypothesis is that useful guides, discoveries and timely news
attract collectors; a matching collection or useful editorial destination then
earns visits and purchases. This is a hypothesis to measure, not guaranteed
growth. Broad reach from a film post can have weaker buying intent than a small
card guide, so measure both without assuming either is worthless.

## Editorial programme

Starting 21-post weekly mix: six news/release posts, six useful collecting/reading
guides, three evidenced market comparisons, three art/history/character stories
and three genuine community choices. Across four weeks, begin with six
artist-focused stories out of 84 posts. This is an experiment allocation; newsworthiness can override it.
Never force a price story where evidence is thin.

| Series | Example angle, not an asserted current headline | Natural destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Behind the Art | A surprising guest artist or visual connection | Exact licensed card guide or matching available collection |
| Build a Binder | Five strong-looking cards within a stated budget | Available cards with price, condition and shipping clarity |
| The Shortlist | Reader-friendly character cards, comic starting points or manga recommendations | Relevant collection or an original reading guide |
| Worth the Grade? | Affordable raw card versus evidenced PSA 10 and PSA 9 | Exact-print raw stock, when present; full comparison methodology |
| The Price Check | Verified price drops, rises or a notable completed sale | Matching live listings; never an automatic investment claim |

| Next on Screen | Confirmed anime, live-action and comic-book movie releases | Release/reading guide with a relevant collecting connection |

## Carousel structure and design

Default 6-8 slides: hook; three to five substantive reveals; practical takeaway;
one next step. Each swipe must add information. Put the strongest useful detail
early; do not stretch three facts across ten slides. A single-image news post is
valid when one image tells the whole story. Do not force every story into a list.

Use six reusable layout structures across the nine editorial series:

1. Hero: 7-12-word hook, one dominant real asset, a small series label.
2. Discovery: card/artifact plus a short connection; preserve exact credits.
3. Comparison: two or three related items, consistent price/identity labels.
4. Evidence: a simple deterministic price table, source/date/sample information.
5. Guide: short reading/release sequence or ranked items with reasons.
6. Close: collector takeaway and one relevant action, with a restrained logo.

Balanced direction: navy, cyan, warm gold and cream; strong typography; generous
space; occasional angled framing around an undistorted card; subtle texture as
an accent. No lime, fake slabs, AI-invented card art, excessive glow, decorative
clutter, giant logos or generic explosion backgrounds. This direction reflects
the founder's preference between the overly decorative and overly plain previews;
it is not final template approval.

Choose 1080x1350 for Instagram/Facebook masters and separately compose 1080x1920
TikTok versions, keeping important details away from UI overlays. These are
design targets, not claims about maximum platform limits. Check each final cover
at phone size, with real text and exact assets, before accepting a template.
AI can draft structured original copy; fixed templates should position text and
licensed assets. Every frame should not be freshly generated by an image model.

Example artist storyboard: original curiosity hook; three independently verified
artist-to-card connections; a visual-style takeaway; a matching guide/collection.
Example film storyboard: confirmed title and region; what is announced; release
date and its status; source-comic/manga context; spoiler-free starter guide;
relevant next step. Do not copy the reference creator's wording/layout wholesale.

## Research, evidence and grading rules

Use social posts as leads; verify the facts independently. Repeated reposts of
one rumour count as one origin, not corroboration. Do not scrape behind login
walls, download/repost other creators' designs or remove watermarks.

Preferred source map: game publishers for card releases; comic/manga publishers
for issue, edition and translation details; studios/distributors and official
anime sites for screen releases. Lorcana [9] and Riftbound [10] have official news
pages. DiscussingFilm and specialist news sites can provide leads; a news source
does not automatically provide licensed marketing imagery or an API/feed.
Record and approve the actual access method per source before unattended fetches.

Release facts need original announcement date, current status, territory,
language, cinema/streaming/print distinction and timezone. A Japanese premiere
is not a UK release date. Recheck before publication. Exclude unconfirmed leaks;
label delays/corrections. Default to spoiler-free covers, with explicit warnings
for any approved story that needs spoilers. Public promotional imagery still
needs a permitted-use check; credit alone does not grant rights.

Price facts need exact printing/variant, language, condition/grade, currency,
sale date, source and fee basis. Distinguish asking, sold and index values.
Comics require edition/issue/printing, cover variant, restoration and grading
company/grade; manga needs volume/edition/language. Do not reuse card assumptions.

Raw-to-graded prototype policy: raw median no more than GBP30; PSA 10 at least
4x raw; at least five distinct completed sales in each of RAW_NM, PSA 9 and PSA 10
within 30 days; same exact printing/language/GBP item-price basis. Flag groups
whose largest price exceeds three times the smallest. These are adjustable
initial editorial filters, not proven optimal thresholds or a grading strategy.
The founder's GBP20/GBP200 example remains illustrative until real evidence qualifies.

Show PSA 9 downside and disclose grading, shipping, selling fees and taxes. No
net profit or expected value is calculated by the prototype. A future cost model
must use current actual costs and defensible probabilities; a population report
does not establish the chance that a raw purchase becomes a PSA 10.

## Traffic, sales and experimentation

Match the destination to the promise. A Lorcana guide should open a relevant
Lorcana collection/guide, not a generic home page. A film headline should lead
to useful adaptation/reading context before any relevant available products.
If there is no suitable live destination, use a save/discussion action; do not
invent stock, preorder availability or a feature that does not exist yet.

Use platform-supported profile/link surfaces. Do not assume a caption URL is
clickable or that TikTok website-link access exists on the actual account.
Build one canonical destination per post with campaign, series, story and
platform identifiers. A profile hub needs recognisable matching post tiles;
it remains a storefront implementation requirement, not a delivered feature.
Where a link passes through a profile hub, preserve the selected tile/story ID.

Measurement chain: post -> profile/link -> tagged visit -> product/guide -> cart
-> order -> refunded-adjusted contribution. Backend order records remain the
financial authority. Keep direct, assisted and unknown attribution separate;
do not count the same order multiple times across platforms. Respect site consent.

Capture own post metrics at 24 hours and 7 days where APIs expose them. Public
competitor saves, shares, reach or swipe completion may be unavailable: null is
not zero. Do not promise per-slide retention for every platform.
Compare series on median saves/reach, shares/reach, qualified visits/1,000 reach,
visit-to-cart/order rates and contribution after content costs. Define a
qualified visit as a product/collection interaction beyond the landing view;
avoid inflated dwell-time-only measurements. This instrumentation is not built yet.

Run a 28-day pilot: weeks 1-2 establish coverage and template baselines; weeks
3-4 rotate two hook/cover alternatives on comparable new stories, changing one
variable at a time. Organic comparisons are observational, not clean randomised
experiments. Report counts, timing, denominators and uncertainty. Treat early series winners as tentative, even when three daily posts increase the
sample: shared topics, timings and audiences make observations dependent. Keep weak results in the dataset.
Never publish near-duplicate posts simply to create an A/B test.

## Implementation delivered in this branch

`backend/app/editorial_preparation.py` prepares 6-8-slide briefs from supplied
facts. It rejects unknown fields, detects missing/social-only/stale evidence,
checks the grading shortlist, separates entertainment media from card grading,
creates a stable content fingerprint and screens destinations against a configured
HTTPS storefront origin. No external URL is fetched. Supplied evidence and prose
remain unverified input; screening does not prove truth, asset rights or stock.

Signed route: `POST /api/v1/automation/commands/editorial/prepare`, using the
existing automation command HMAC and 64KiB request limit. Configure
`TCG_EDITORIAL_STOREFRONT_ORIGIN` to the verified public origin before link
preparation; leave unset to suppress commerce links. This is not a production
configuration change. Responses always say `publishable:false`, `stored:false`.
Status is `PREPARED_FOR_REVIEW` or `NEEDS_EVIDENCE`, never published.

Inactive n8n file: `automation/n8n/workflows/dr-31-static-editorial-preparation.json`.
It accepts a `brief_json` string, signs a call to the real implemented endpoint,
validates the nonpublishing response and returns it. No schedule, live research
feed, AI call, renderer, social credentials or success receipt is attached.
Transport failures use the existing DR-90 error route once deployed/proven.
The content fingerprint is not a durable publication idempotency lock.

The existing four-platform design JSON is still a design document, not an n8n
export or a runtime-consumed configuration. Registry families remain DESIGNED
because a preparation component is not the full creative/publishing family.
No live import/deploy, social post, subscription, data migration or spend is made.
