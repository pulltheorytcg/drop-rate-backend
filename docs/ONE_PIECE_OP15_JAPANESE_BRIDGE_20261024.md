# One Piece OP15 Japanese catalogue guide recovery — 24 October 2026

## Scope

The existing Japanese One Piece regional guide importer only accepted an
English Punk Records checklist counterpart. The published English OP15 base
checklist is instead supplied by Bandai Official, whose rarity labels are
abbreviations (C, UC, R, SR, L, SEC). The corresponding independent Japanese
Punk Records checklist spells out those rarities.

The importer now admits **only** the exact observed English
`BOOSTER PACK -ADVENTURE ON KAMI’S ISLAND- [OP15-EB04]` to Japanese
`ブースターパック 神の島の冒険【OP-15】` release pair. Each guide requires:

- Exact OP15 base provider ID and printed card number in both checklists;
- An explicitly enumerated equal rarity (never an inferred variant);
- A unique region-labelled Cardmarket expansion and unique card-number/name
  product; every alternate/duplicate product blocks the match;
- A current positive first-party Cardmarket guide and dated ECB EUR-to-GBP FX.

The result remains labelled `MIXED_NON_ENGLISH_REFERENCE`, is not an exact
Japanese sold valuation, and is **never** eligible for physical-copy pricing,
owner balances, Store Price or Shopify repricing. Unknown abbreviations and
other Bandai release titles remain excluded until independently verified.

identities (4,527 / 4,480) were replayed against Cardmarket's official
2026-10-24 game-18 singles, non-singles and price guide exports.

- Old matcher: 1,424 Japanese reference matches.
- Guarded matcher: 1,517 matches; **93 newly eligible positive guides**.
- Existing matches lost: **0**.
- New matches are all the OP15 Japanese base release; no premium reprint,
  promo, alternate art or other game/language is promoted.
- The publisher and marketplace product strings were independently compared;
  a full suite of positive and negative fixtures checks source collisions,
  rarity, numbers, set pair, region, variant and zero/incorrect quotes.

The source replay is **not** by itself a live data backfill. A release and
bounded production backfill, if performed, must be separately recorded after
transactional identity/source/FX, capacity, inventory and history verification.
The full catalogue 90% target remains unmet even after 93 new quotes.
