# 12. Buyer purchase → Seller Hub portfolio requirement

This requirement was explicitly added to the backlog on 30 September and is also represented as workflows 24/25 in the automation registry.

Desired customer flow:

1. customer purchases on Shopify and provides an email;
2. customer is invited/encouraged to create a Drop Rate Seller Hub account;
3. account linking uses verified identity/email rather than loose string matching;
4. purchased card(s) appear in the customer's portfolio after the sale reaches the appropriate verified state;
5. the customer gets normal Seller Hub portfolio/scanner functions immediately.

Critical architecture rule:

**A portfolio record is not permission to rewrite historical sale/owner data.**

The implementation must distinguish:

- inventory ownership before sale;
- the completed order and previous seller/consignor settlement;

# 18. Architecture decisions reaffirmed

The work in this window did **not** change the core platform architecture.

- One unified Shopify storefront.
- PostgreSQL/Supabase is master truth.
- Canonical Card/Product identity remains separate from physical Inventory Item.
- Every physical item keeps a unique Inventory ID and explicit owner.
- FastAPI owns deterministic pricing/ownership/finance/security rules.
- n8n orchestrates, schedules and moves data; it does not become the database or rules engine.
- AI may identify, interpret, generate and prioritise, but cannot silently override identity, ownership, pricing or settlement truth.
- Market-data providers remain adapter-based and ToS/permission-aware.
- Duplicate webhooks/workflows must be idempotent.
- Historical observations/audit events are not destructively overwritten.
- External seller/consignor access remains least-privilege and owner-isolated.
- Human effort should concentrate on physical handling, acquisitions, relationships, strategy and genuine exceptions.
